## 🎬 Recording Tips

### Pre-Recording Setup
1. Close unnecessary browser tabs
2. Clear browser console
3. Restart backend and frontend for fresh demo
4. Test audio levels
5. Enable "Do Not Disturb" mode

### During Recording
- **Pace**: Slow down—speak 20% slower than normal
- **Energy**: Enthusiastic but not over-the-top
- **Pauses**: 2-second pause after each section for editing
- **Mouse**: Smooth, deliberate movements
- **Clicks**: Emphasize important clicks with a brief pause

### Screen Recording Settings
- **Tool**: OBS Studio (free) or Loom
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080
- **FPS**: 30fps
- **Audio**: External mic (not laptop mic)
- **Area**: Full screen or centered window with context
